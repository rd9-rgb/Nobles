THE RESIDENCE

A private address, built without compromise

Nobles Residence stands at Strada Barajul Argeș 36, a quiet residential street in the Șoseaua Nordului area — in northern Sector 1, close to Herăstrău. Sixty apartments — two, three and four rooms, and a collection of penthouses — are arranged around a restrained, contemporary façade, finished throughout with natural stone, brushed brass and European oak. THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

The Șoseaua Nordului area, where Herăstrău Park meets the city

Beyond the boulevards, in the Șoseaua Nordului area, the city slows down around Herăstrău.

The Șoseaua Nordului area is one of the most sought-after places to live in Bucharest, an address that adds lasting value to every home here. Wrapped around the lake and gardens of Herăstrău Park, it is a landscape of old trees, quiet waterside paths and villas that have kept their character for a century. Streets here are narrower, greener, and largely spared the city's rush; mornings arrive through curtains of trees rather than traffic.

Strada Barajul Argeș 36 sits within this calm, a short reach from the park, the embassies and the metro lines that make the Șoseaua Nordului area and Herăstrău the address of choice for those who want the city near, not on top of them.

It is a neighbourhood of ambassadors and old money, of long walks along the lake and short commutes — where privacy is not a luxury but simply how the street was built.

FINISHES & SPECIFICATIONS

Quality You Can See. Comfort You Can Feel.

Considered, down to the details

At NOBLES RESIDENCE, quality is considered from the very beginning — from the architecture and technical systems of the building to the materials, finishes and details that shape each home.

01

HEATING SYSTEM

NOBLES RESIDENCE benefits from a centralised heating system serving the entire building, powered by two high-performance De Dietrich heating units located in the technical area at basement level. Heat consumption is individually metered for each apartment, combining the efficiency and reliability of a central building system with individual control over consumption. Heating within the apartments is provided through a combination of convectors and Purmo radiators, ensuring efficient and comfortable heat distribution throughout each home.

02

AIR CONDITIONING & CLIMATE CONTROL

Each apartment is equipped with an individual DAIKIN climate-control system designed to provide both cooling and heating. The system is centrally distributed within each apartment, allowing conditioned air to be delivered discreetly throughout the interior while preserving the clean architectural design of the living spaces. Each residence has its own dedicated outdoor DAIKIN unit, positioned on the upper roof of the building. This solution keeps external equipment away from balconies and façades while providing each apartment with an independent climate-control system. Together with the building’s central heating system, the DAIKIN installation provides additional flexibility and year-round thermal comfort.

03

WINDOWS & EXTERIOR JOINERY

The exterior joinery has been selected to provide generous natural light, thermal performance and a clean contemporary appearance. NOBLES RESIDENCE incorporates premium SCHÜCO aluminium systems, selected according to the architectural and technical requirements of each façade element. Large glazed surfaces enhance the connection between the interiors and outdoor spaces while contributing to the contemporary architectural identity of the residence.

04

ELEVATORS

Each staircase is served by a SCHINDLER 3300 elevator with a capacity of up to 9 persons. Designed for smooth and reliable operation, the elevators combine performance with elegant cabin finishes, ensuring comfort from the moment residents enter the building to the moment they arrive home.

05

DOORS

Apartment entrance doors combine security with an elegant contemporary appearance, featuring a metallic structure with refined wood-effect finishes. Interior wooden doors are supplied by PINUM, with a selection of finishes available to complement the interior design of each residence.

06

FLOORING

Living areas and bedrooms are designed to feature premium natural wood flooring, selected to bring warmth, character and timeless elegance to each home. To allow a degree of personalization, owners may choose their preferred wood tone and finish from a curated selection, carefully chosen to complement the contemporary interior design of NOBLES RESIDENCE. allowing each residence to reflect the individual character of its future owner while preserving the overall design identity and quality standards of the project.

07

CERAMIC TILES & BATHROOMS

Ceramic surfaces are finished with carefully selected tiles from renowned Italian manufacturers, chosen to complement the contemporary character of the apartments. Depending on the stage of completion and the available selection, owners may choose from a curated range of colours and finishes to personalise their future home. Bathrooms feature quality sanitary ware and fittings from established brands combining refined contemporary design with everyday functionality.

08

BALCONIES, TERRACES & LANDSCAPING

Outdoor spaces are an integral part of the NOBLES RESIDENCE concept. Balconies incorporate durable REHAU WPC decking, selected for its contemporary appearance and suitability for outdoor use. Landscaped green areas with automatic irrigation complement the architecture and contribute to a calm residential atmosphere. Selected ground-floor apartments benefit from private planted gardens and terraces, extending the living space outdoors and offering an additional distinction in the heart of Șoseaua Nordului..

09

ELECTRICAL FITTINGS & VIDEO INTERCOM

Electrical switches and sockets throughout the apartments are from the premium BTicino Living Now collection, selected for their minimalist contemporary design, refined finishes and seamless integration with the interiors. The apartments feature the Black finish, carefully selected to complement the warm wood, neutral surfaces and darker architectural details of the NOBLES RESIDENCE interior concept. Each apartment is also equipped with a video intercom system, allowing convenient visitor identification and controlled access directly from the residence.

10

SECURITY & COMMON AREAS

The common areas of NOBLES RESIDENCE have been designed as an extension of the residential experience, combining contemporary design, comfort and security. The residence features an elegant entrance lobby, 24-hour security and high-resolution CCTV . Energy-efficient LED lighting is integrated throughout the building and common spaces, providing comfortable illumination while reducing energy consumption.
